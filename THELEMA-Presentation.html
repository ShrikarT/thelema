  • No Asset Pricing: Cannot price how Nvidia, tech equities, or commodities react to the decision.

What Capital Actually Needs (THELEMA)

  • Conditional Synthetic Assets: Outcome-contingent shares: sNVDA if policy passes vs sNVDA if policy fails.
